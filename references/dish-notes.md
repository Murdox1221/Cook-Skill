# Dish notes

Shopping-relevant ingredients per dish, used to build grocery lists. Assumed
pantry staples (see `kitchen-profile.md`) are left out unless a dish needs an
unusual amount.

Quantities below assume ~2 servings with leftovers — scale to the run.

**Some of these are best guesses.** Lines marked _[confirm]_ are family-specific
or ambiguous — ask for the real recipe and update this file. Lines marked
_[sub]_ replace an excluded ingredient (mushrooms, chunked tomato).

---

## Hot dogs, hamburgers, & fries
Hot dogs, hot dog buns, hamburger buns, sliced American cheese, frozen fries.
(Ground beef = backbone.)

## Sausage & potatoes
Smoked sausage / kielbasa (1 pkg), potatoes (~1.5 lb), 1 bell pepper.

## Chicken Alfredo
Fettuccine, heavy cream (1 pt), block Parmesan, optional broccoli crown.
(Chicken breast = backbone.)

## Pulled pork sliders, potato salad, fries
Pork shoulder/butt (2–3 lb), BBQ sauce, slider/Hawaiian rolls, frozen fries,
prepared potato salad — or make it: extra potatoes, eggs, sweet pickle relish.

## Chicken fajitas
2–3 bell peppers, flour tortillas, sour cream, shredded Mexican cheese, fajita
seasoning. Optional: smooth salsa, guacamole. (Chicken breast = backbone.)

## Mac & cheese with hot dogs
Elbow macaroni + block cheddar + milk, or 2 boxes mac & cheese; hot dogs.

## Homemade nachos with beef, chicken, or steak
Tortilla chips, shredded cheese or nacho cheese sauce, 1 can black beans,
jalapeños, sour cream, green onion. Steak (if using): ~1 lb sirloin.
(Beef / chicken = backbone.)

## Forgotten chicken
Long-grain white rice, 1 can cream of chicken soup, 1 can cream of celery soup
_[sub: replaces cream of mushroom]_, 1 pkt onion soup mix. (Chicken breast = backbone.)

## Crunch wrap
Burrito-size flour tortillas, tostada shells, nacho cheese sauce, shredded
lettuce, sour cream, shredded cheese. Skip diced tomato _[sub]_ or use smooth
salsa. (Ground beef = backbone.)

## Chicken fried rice
Cook rice a day ahead. Frozen peas & carrots, green onion, sesame oil, eggs.
(Chicken breast = backbone.) _[confirm portions]_

## Tater casserole
Frozen tater tots, shredded cheddar, 1 can cream of chicken soup _[sub: replaces
cream of mushroom]_, 1 bag frozen green beans or corn. (Ground beef = backbone.)
_[confirm — this is the tater-tot hotdish version]_

## Enchiladas
Flour or corn tortillas, 1 can smooth red or green enchilada sauce, shredded
cheese, 1 can black beans, sour cream, green onion. (Chicken or ground beef = backbone.)

## Tacos, rice, & beans
Taco shells and/or tortillas, taco seasoning, shredded cheese, lettuce, sour
cream, 1 can refried or black beans, boxed Mexican/Spanish rice (or rice +
smooth tomato sauce). (Ground beef = backbone.)

## Buffalo Mac
Cavatappi or elbow pasta, buffalo/hot sauce, ranch or blue cheese dressing,
block cheddar, cream cheese or extra milk. (Chicken breast = backbone.)

## Vegetable pot roast
Carrots (1 lb), potatoes (~2 lb), celery, parsnip/turnip optional. _[confirm:
does this include a chuck roast, or is it meatless?]_

## Walking taco
Individual snack bags of Fritos or Doritos (one per person), taco seasoning,
shredded cheese, shredded lettuce, sour cream, diced onion. Skip tomato _[sub]_.
(Ground beef = backbone.)

## Teriyaki chicken
Teriyaki sauce (or soy + brown sugar + fresh ginger), white rice, broccoli or
frozen stir-fry vegetables, sesame seeds. (Chicken breast = backbone.)

## Quesadillas
Flour tortillas, shredded cheese, sour cream. Optional smooth salsa.
(Chicken or ground beef = backbone.)

## Chili Mac
Elbow macaroni, 1 can tomato sauce + 1 small can tomato paste _[smooth — no
diced tomato]_, 1 can kidney or chili beans, shredded cheddar. (Ground beef = backbone.)

## Chili with meat
1 can tomato sauce + 1 small can tomato paste _[smooth — no diced tomato]_,
2 cans kidney/pinto beans, shredded cheddar, sour cream, corn chips optional.
Optional stew beef (~1 lb) in addition to ground beef. (Beef = backbone.)

## Spaghetti & garlic toast
Spaghetti, 1 jar smooth marinara / pasta sauce + 1 small can tomato paste
_[smooth]_, block Parmesan, frozen garlic bread or Texas toast. (Ground beef = backbone.)

## Salad
Romaine or mixed greens, cucumber, carrots, 1 bell pepper, red onion, croutons,
shredded cheese, dressing. Optional grilled chicken breast. No tomato.

## Pizza & mozzarella sticks
2 frozen pizzas (or crusts + smooth pizza sauce + mozzarella + pepperoni),
1 box frozen mozzarella sticks, marinara dip.

## Stuffed bell peppers
4 large bell peppers, 1 can tomato sauce _[smooth]_, cooked rice, shredded
cheese. (Ground beef = backbone.)

## Breakfast (pancakes, eggs, bacon, hash browns)
Pancake mix + syrup, bacon, eggs, frozen hash browns / shredded potatoes.

## Breakfast burritos
Burrito-size flour tortillas, eggs, breakfast sausage or bacon, shredded cheese,
frozen hash browns / potatoes, 1 bell pepper. Optional smooth salsa.

## Steak, mashed potatoes, green beans
Steaks (sirloin or ribeye, one per person), potatoes (~2 lb), green beans
(fresh, frozen, or canned).

## Pork chops, mashed potatoes, green beans
Pork chops (one per person), potatoes (~2 lb), green beans.

## Meatloaf with mashed potatoes & green beans
Plain breadcrumbs, 1 egg, ketchup or smooth tomato sauce for glaze, potatoes
(~2 lb), green beans. (Ground beef = backbone.)

## Porcupine meatballs
Long-grain white rice (uncooked, mixed into meatballs), 1 can tomato sauce +
1 can tomato soup or paste _[smooth]_, 1 egg. (Ground beef = backbone.)

## Sloppy joes & fries
Hamburger buns, 1 can Manwich-style sloppy joe sauce _[smooth]_ (or 1 can tomato
sauce + 1 small can tomato paste + brown sugar), frozen fries. Optional 1 green
bell pepper. (Ground beef = backbone.)

## BBQ chicken, cornbread & corn
2 boxes corn muffin / cornbread mix, corn (1 can or 1 bag frozen). BBQ sauce is a
staple — check the level, it also gets used on pulled pork. (Chicken breast = backbone.)

## Chicken tenders & fries
1 bag frozen breaded chicken tenders (or bread your own: panko, 1 egg, flour),
frozen fries, dipping sauces (staples). Optional bagged slaw or veg.

## Hamburger steak with onion gravy
2 large onions, beef broth (staple), plain breadcrumbs, 1 egg. Serve over mashed
potatoes (potatoes ~2 lb, milk/butter staple). No mushrooms in the gravy.
(Ground beef = backbone.)

## French dip sandwiches
1–1.5 lb thin-sliced deli roast beef (or a small chuck roast), hoagie / sub rolls,
provolone slices, 1 onion, 1 packet au jus mix (or beef broth + Worcestershire).

## Baked ziti
1 lb ziti, 1 jar smooth marinara _[smooth]_ + 1 small can tomato paste, 1 tub
ricotta, 1 bag shredded mozzarella, block Parmesan. (Ground beef = backbone, optional.)

## Goulash (American, elbow mac)
1 box elbow macaroni, 1 can tomato sauce + 1 small can tomato paste _[smooth]_,
beef broth (staple), 1 bag shredded cheddar, onion (staple). (Ground beef = backbone.)

## Taco salad
Tortilla chips or corn chips, shredded lettuce, shredded cheese, 1 can black or
kidney beans, sour cream, Catalina / French dressing, green onion. No tomato.
(Ground beef = backbone.)

## Taco soup
1 can tomato sauce _[smooth — replaces diced tomatoes / Ro-Tel]_, 1 small can
diced green chiles, 1 can corn, 1 can black beans, 1 can kidney or pinto beans,
1 packet ranch seasoning, taco seasoning (staple). (Ground beef = backbone.)

## Beef & bean burritos
Burrito-size flour tortillas, 1 can refried beans, shredded cheese, sour cream,
smooth salsa. Optional Spanish rice. (Ground beef = backbone.)
