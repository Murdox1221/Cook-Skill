# Kitchen profile

Edit this file to change what the meal planner assumes. It is read at the start
of every run.

## Proteins usually in stock

- Ground beef
- Chicken breast

Most dinners are built around these two. Do **not** add them to the grocery list
unless a run explicitly says stock is low.

## Other proteins bought per-week

These show up in the rotation and **do** go on the grocery list: hot dogs,
smoked sausage / kielbasa, breakfast sausage, bacon, pork shoulder (pulled
pork), pork chops, steak (sirloin/ribeye), pepperoni.

## Never use

- **Tomatoes in pieces** — whole, halved, diced, crushed-with-chunks, cherry,
  grape, sun-dried, fresh sliced. Canned diced/whole tomatoes are out.
- **Smooth tomato products ARE allowed** and appear all over the rotation
  (enchilada sauce, chili, chili mac, spaghetti sauce, pizza sauce, stuffed
  peppers, porcupine meatballs): tomato paste, tomato sauce, tomato soup,
  passata, ketchup, smooth salsa, marinara without visible pieces.
- **Mushrooms** — any variety, fresh or dried, mushroom broth, and
  **cream of mushroom soup**. Substitute cream of chicken or cream of celery in
  casseroles (Forgotten chicken, Tater casserole).
- **Seafood** — fish, shellfish, squid, anchovy, fish sauce, shrimp paste,
  oyster sauce. Worcestershire sauce is fine.

## FFY nights

"Fend for yourself." No dinner is cooked and nothing is bought for it. Skip FFY
nights entirely when building the grocery list. Optionally keep easy FFY
fallbacks stocked (frozen pizza, sandwich fixings, ramen, leftovers).

## Pantry staples assumed on hand

Not added to the grocery list unless a recipe needs an unusual quantity.

- Salt, black pepper, cooking oil, olive oil, butter, non-stick spray
- Onions, garlic, potatoes (usually), eggs, milk
- White and brown rice, dried pasta (spaghetti, elbow), flour, cornstarch, sugar
- Ketchup, mustard, mayo, BBQ sauce, soy sauce, hot sauce, ranch, vinegar,
  Worcestershire, syrup
- Chicken / beef broth or bouillon
- Dried spices: taco/fajita seasoning, chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic
  powder, onion powder, Italian seasoning, oregano, ground ginger, bay leaf,
  red pepper flakes
- Canned beans (black, kidney, refried), canned corn, breadcrumbs
